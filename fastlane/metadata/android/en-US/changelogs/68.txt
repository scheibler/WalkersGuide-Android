- New: Choose between different route profiles such as "Shortest route" or "On foot in urban areas" to customize the calculated route to your preferences. The latter prefers smaller streets and accepts a detour. In the app settings, you can also create your own route profiles or edit existing ones, as well as set a default route profile. Then you won't be asked every time when calculating a route.
- New: Have the intersection layout announced during navigation. This gives you a better idea of the streets to cross when approaching an intersection. This option is disabled by default for now. If you want to try out the announcements, you can find the "Speak intersection structure" switch in the app settings under the "Text to speech" section.
- New: TalkBack actions for frequently used context menu entries in the point list: "Navigate to this point" and "Start simulation"
- New: Menu entry in the "More options" menu of the point list to toggle the "Auto-update" and "Filter by viewing direction" functions together on or off.
- Fixed: Departure data for stops served by Germany's VVO provider is now loading correctly again.
- Fixed: Entered addresses are now formatted correctly again
